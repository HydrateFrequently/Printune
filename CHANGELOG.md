# Changelog

What changed in each update of Printune's main branch, newest first.

## 2026-10-17

- **Belt tension module updated** to the same analysis as the Chordata plugin:
  - fans and other steady hums are kept out of the belt reading;
  - the toolhead's notes can be learned from several taps;
  - a toolhead note higher than the accelerometer hears well is flagged;
  - a measurement holding two plucks is refused instead of giving a false reading.

## 2026-10-15

- First public release.

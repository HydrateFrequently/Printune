Machine setup

Printune prints several tests on one plate, one after the other. While it prints a later test, the toolhead and the X gantry move next to and over the earlier ones. A finished print that is taller than the gap under the toolhead (the nozzle protrusion) or under the gantry gets hit: prints knocked off, a bent nozzle, skipped steps or worse. Printune places the tests with the numbers below. It assumes none of them: they are yours to measure.

1Bed area: where the nozzle may print (mm)

2Toolhead box: from the nozzle to each side (mm)

The whole toolhead: fans, ducts, probe, carriage, cable mounts. The X gantry is taken to reach as far in Y as this box: if the beam or anything under it sticks out further in Y, use that.

How to measure the toolhead box
  1. -X is the side where the X position gets smaller when you move the toolhead, +X where it gets larger; the same for Y. Check it with the move buttons, don't guess front and back.
  2. Look at the toolhead from above and find the part that sticks out furthest on each side.
  3. Measure from the centre of the nozzle tip to that part with a ruler or calipers held flat under the toolhead. Round up by a few mm.
  4. Or with the printer: stand a box with a straight edge on the bed. Move the toolhead in X until its side just touches the box and note X. Raise Z, move until the nozzle tip is right above the box's edge and note X again. The difference is that side's distance. The same for the other sides.

From the bed to the underside of the X gantry (the beam the toolhead rides on, with its brackets and anything hanging below it, not the toolhead) with the nozzle at Z=0.

How to measure the gantry height
  1. Home the printer with your Z offset set, and move to Z=50.
  2. Measure from the bed surface straight up to the lowest point of the X gantry.
  3. Take 50 off and round down by a couple of mm. Example: 92 mm at Z=50 gives 42 mm minus a margin.

From the nozzle tip up to the lowest other part of the toolhead: fan duct, probe, silicone sock, a screw head, anything that hangs down. A finished print at least 0.2 mm lower than this may sit under the toolhead while it prints next to it (the nozzle never goes below the first layer, so the real gap is bigger); a taller one is kept outside the toolhead box.

How to measure the nozzle protrusion
  1. Let the hotend cool down. Lay a flat, stiff card on the bed (a business card or an old bank card).
  2. Lower the nozzle in small steps until its tip just touches the card.
  3. Look at the toolhead from the side and find its lowest part other than the nozzle: the fan duct, the probe (a pin probe with its pin up; its body counts), the silicone sock, a screw head.
  4. Measure from the card straight up to that part with the depth rod of your calipers, or slide stacked cards under it until they touch. Take off the card's thickness only if you measured from the bed.
  5. Or off the printer: calipers from the nozzle tip up to the bottom of that part.
  6. Round down by a few tenths, and measure again after a new nozzle, duct, sock or probe.
5Your responsibility

Saved under [printune] in printer.cfg with SAVE_CONFIG, without a restart. If other changes are waiting for SAVE_CONFIG (a new Z offset, for example), it does not save: run SAVE_CONFIG yourself then, which saves them and the machine setup together and restarts, so nothing is written that the printer isn't running with. Changing a value, here or by hand in printer.cfg, means accepting the risk again. Change it any time here or with _PRINTUNE_SETUP in the console.
